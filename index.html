Softshade

Games made by Softshade and friends.

  • Pokémon Sunday GitHub A full-length fan game, about 70 hours of play, built on Pokémon Essentials and set in its own region, Kiraki: sixteen routes, eight gyms, an Elite Four, a Battle Tower, and twenty-three Pokémon original to the game. Runs on Windows, Mac, Linux and Android.
  • Floradiem A daily plant identification game. Study photos of a British Columbia plant, fungus or seaweed and work down the tree of life from kingdom to species, learning what sets each group apart.
  • Worm Game A daily word ladder puzzle. Get from the start word to the goal word by adding, removing, or changing one letter at a time, keeping every step a valid English word.
